How to Play

You're Player 1, fixed as Industry Captain (🔫 Gun / ✈️ Plane). The CPU is Player 2, fixed as Party Boss (🌾 Sickle / 🔨 Hammer) — it rolls, charges, plays cards and ends its own turns automatically. Reduce your opponent's GDP counter from 40 to 0 to win.

◆ Your Faction

At the start of the game — and again the moment your turn ends — your faction is tapped. While tapped, a roll can only untap it: land 2 or more of your own two symbols among the 3 faction dice (any combination) to untap. Nothing else happens on a tapped roll — no draws, no charging — until you're untapped.

◆ Rolling

Tap the dice table to roll all 4 dice — three faction dice (🌾 Sickle, 🔨 Hammer, 🔫 Gun, ✈️ Plane, ⚔️ Sword, 🛡️ Shield) plus an equip die (I / II / ○ / X). You get up to 3 rolls a turn: tap any individual die to lock it (a ring lights up) so it's kept for your next roll, then tap elsewhere to reroll everything still unlocked. If every die is already locked, tapping again ends your cycle early — same as running out of rolls. Rolling an X on the equip die busts you instantly — your turn ends right there, no card play allowed.

◆ Matches & Draws

Once untapped, 2 of your 3 faction dice showing your own two symbols draws you a card. Land all 3 and the equip die adds more: I = +1 extra draw, II = +2, ○ and X add nothing extra. A lone ⚔️ Sword rolled charges a Ballistic Missile in your hand; a lone 🛡️ Shield-face rolled charges a Shield card in your hand — each is a no-op if you're not holding the matching card.

◆ Ballistic Missile & Shield

Ballistic Missile — tap the card, or roll matching Sword dice, 3 times to charge it. The 3rd charge fires it automatically: a bomb die rolls 0/10/15/20 damage straight to your opponent, and knocks every table die off for the rest of that turn. Kept for the whole game — only one per player.

Shield — tap the card, or roll matching Shield-face dice, 3 times to arm it. Once armed it stays in your hand for good: every hit against you is halved and spends one charge, and further Shield-face rolls or taps recharge it. Only a Missile ever destroys it (still halving that hit first), sending it back into the deck at random.

◆ Cards

Both players draw from one shared 40-card deck. Your hand caps at 4 — tap a card in your hand to play it. Damage and Restore cards apply straight to the GDP counters; everything else resolves its own printed text (see the Card Gallery). Once the deck runs dry, the discard pile reshuffles back in.

◆ Ending Your Turn

Once your 3-roll cycle ends, you may play as many cards as you like — then tap End Turn (⏭️) yourself. With an empty hand, your turn advances automatically instead.
