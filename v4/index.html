How to Play

You (Player 1) and the CPU (Player 2) alternate turns automatically — the CPU rolls, recruits, declares, and attacks on its own. Each round has two phases: the Recruitment, where you recruit factions onto your roster, and the Battle, where your rosters fight. Best of three rounds wins.

◆ The Recruitment

On your turn, tap the standing stones to cast four dice: three faction dice — 🌾 Sickle, 🔨 Hammer, 🔫 Gun, ✈️ Plane, ⚔️ Sword, 📰 Propaganda — plus the equip die (I/II/III/O/X). You get 3 rolls total: tap a die for a golden ring to lock it, then tap anywhere else to reroll the rest. Tap elsewhere again once you're happy (or once your rerolls run out) to lock in the result.

  • 3 Sickle/Hammer, in any combination → Party Boss
  • 3 Gun/Plane, in any combination → Industry Captain
  • 3 Sword → Knight
  • A lone Sword or 📰 Propaganda can stand in as a wildcard third die for Party Boss or Industry Captain — 2 Sickle/Hammer plus 1 Sword or Propaganda still claims Party Boss, and likewise for Industry Captain. Knight never accepts a substitute — it needs all 3 Sword.
  • 📰 Propaganda doesn't form a faction of its own. Instead: land 2 Propaganda dice and draw 2 cards, or land all 3 and draw 3 — whether or not a claim forms. Roll exactly 1 Propaganda and it completes a claim as the wildcard, and you draw 1 card too.

Land a claim and that faction joins your roster immediately. Roll an X alongside a claim and the recruit never arrives — the recruitment passes quietly to your opponent either way if no claim forms.

Once you've recruited someone this phase, you may Declare Battle — but only once 3 turns have passed since whoever recruited first in the phase did so. That gives the other player a real window to catch up before anyone can lock in a fight. Whoever actually declares first attacks first, and if the other side still hasn't recruited anything by then, the battle goes ahead anyway — one-sided. A card draw is often your best shot at climbing back from that.

Every claim — first time or a repeat — is worth a number of recruits set by the equip die: I = 1, II = 2, III = 3, O = 0. Where those recruits go depends on the 3 faction dice: Plane and Hammer lean attack, Gun and Sickle lean defense, and Sword/Propaganda carry their own lean (attack/defense) even when rolled as the wildcard 3rd die. Whichever type has the majority of the 3 dice gets all of that roll's recruits — so Knight (always 3 Sword) banks pure attack recruits, automatically. Rolling a faction you already have doesn't add a second unit, it reinforces the one you've got. Recruits aren't capped — watch the little numbers on each faction tile in your roster.

◆ The Battle

Tap one of your units to attack with it. Your attack points are simply that faction's attack recruits plus whatever the attack die shows: ×1 adds nothing, +1/+2/+3 add that much. Facing an actual defender wears your attacking faction down by 1 attack recruit, same as defending wears the defender down — but attacking a side with no units left costs you nothing.

If the defender has any units summoned, they choose which one defends — even a unit that's already attacked this exchange can still defend. That faction's banked defense recruits set the maximum number of times the defense die can be rolled. When it's your own defense, you choose before each roll whether to keep rolling or tap ✋ Stop to bank whatever's left — anything you don't spend stays with that faction for a later attack, this battle or the next recruit-then-battle exchange. Each roll you do take spends one of those recruits either way. On a plain 1/2/3, that many points of the incoming attack are absorbed and never reach your health. On a burst (🔆), nothing is absorbed, but half the attack (rounded up) reflects straight back at the attacker instead. Whatever damage isn't absorbed after you're done rolling reduces the defender's health.

Reduce your opponent to 0 to win the round. First to two round wins takes the match.

◆ Cards

You draw from a shared 40-card deck (2 to start) and may play as many cards as you like on your turn — playing a card no longer refills your hand on its own, so there's no draw-based reason to hold back. Instead, rolling 📰 Propaganda is your main source of new cards during recruitment. Your hand can hold up to 5 at once; drawing past that prompts a discard. At the start of every new recruiting phase, your hand resets to exactly 2: discard down if you're holding more, draw up if you're holding fewer. If the deck ever runs dry, the discard pile reshuffles back in. Tap a card in the hand bar at the bottom for its full effect — damage, healing, binding an opponent's recruitment turns, stealing or destroying units, extra attack/defense rolls, drawing, revealing the next deck card alongside one of your opponent's to take one or the other, Fortune's Grasp (bumps every recruitment roll you take up to 4 rolls instead of the usual 3, lasting until the next battle phase begins), or The Flood, a battle-only card that wipes every unit from both rosters at once. Some cards are recruitment-only or battle-only; the bar shows whichever player's turn it currently is.
