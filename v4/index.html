How to Play

You (Player 1) and the CPU (Player 2) alternate turns automatically — the CPU rolls, recruits, declares, and attacks on its own. Each round has two phases: the Recruitment, where you recruit factions onto your roster, and the Battle, where your rosters fight. Best of three rounds wins.

◆ The Recruitment

On your turn, tap the standing stones to cast four dice: three faction dice — 🌾 Sickle, 🔨 Hammer, 🔫 Gun, ✈️ Plane, ⚔️ Sword, 🛸 UFO — plus the equip die (I/II/III/O/X). You get 3 rolls total: tap a die for a golden ring to lock it, then tap anywhere else to reroll the rest. Tap elsewhere again once you're happy (or once your rerolls run out) to lock in the result.

  • 3 Sickle/Hammer, in any combination → Party Boss
  • 3 Gun/Plane, in any combination → Industry Captain
  • 3 Sword → Knight
  • 3 UFO → Prefect Zorb
  • A lone Sword or UFO can also stand in as a wildcard third die for Party Boss or Industry Captain — 2 Sickle/Hammer plus 1 Sword or UFO still claims Party Boss, and likewise for Industry Captain. Knight and Prefect Zorb never accept a substitute — they need all 3 of their own symbol.

Land a claim and that faction joins your roster immediately, with its power set straight off the equip die: I = 1, II = 2, III = 3, O = 0. Roll an X alongside a claim and the recruit never arrives — the recruitment passes quietly to your opponent either way if no claim forms.

Once you've recruited someone this phase, you may Declare Battle. Whoever declares first attacks first — though nothing happens until your opponent has recruited someone too.

◆ The Battle

Tap one of your units to attack with it. A 4-sided die (🔆, 1, 2, 3 — one face each) rolls for your attack: its value plus your unit's power always lands as damage.

If the defender has any units summoned, their side rolls the same 4-sided die right back. A burst reflects a counter-hit onto you — half your damage, rounded up, or 1 if you dealt none. Roll a plain number that matches or beats the attacking unit's power, and the attack still lands in full, but the attacking unit is destroyed outright. Anything else and the defense does nothing to stop the blow.

Knight units get an extra attack roll whenever they strike. Any Prefect Zorb unit on the defending roster grants that whole side an extra defense roll on every incoming attack, no matter which unit is actually under attack.

Reduce your opponent to 0 to win the round. First to two round wins takes the match.

◆ Cards

You draw from a shared 40-card deck (2 to start, then one fresh card every time you play one — capped at 3 in hand, so playing while full still refills you back up to 3), and may play one per turn. If the deck ever runs dry, the discard pile reshuffles back in. Tap a card in the hand bar at the bottom for its full effect — damage, healing, binding an opponent's recruitment turns, stealing or destroying units, extra attack/defense rolls, drawing, revealing the next deck card alongside one of your opponent's to take one or the other, Fortune's Grasp (bumps every recruitment roll you take up to 4 rolls instead of the usual 3, lasting until the next battle phase begins), or The Flood, a battle-only card that wipes every unit from both rosters at once. Some cards are recruitment-only or battle-only; the bar shows whichever player's turn it currently is.
